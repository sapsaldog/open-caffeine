# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Open Caffeine to be a welcoming and respectful project for everyone,
regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Be kind and constructive. Assume good intent, give and accept feedback
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its community.
Conduct that makes others feel unwelcome or unsafe is not acceptable.

## Adopted standard

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. Please read the
full text for the complete expectations and enforcement guidelines:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Enforcement

Concerns can be reported privately to the maintainer at **sapsaldog@gmail.com**.
All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and may restrict participants who do not follow
this Code of Conduct.
