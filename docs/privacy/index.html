Privacy Policy for Open Caffeine

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Open Caffeine is a macOS utility developed by Donghun Choi. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Core app functionality

Open Caffeine does not require an account. Its core keep-awake functionality and preferences operate locally on your Mac. We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Optional feedback

Open Caffeine includes an optional Feedback feature. Nothing is transmitted through this feature unless you choose to submit feedback. A submission may include:

  • a rating and written comment;
  • an email address, if you choose to provide one;
  • up to three images that you choose to attach;
  • the app version and build number, macOS version, locale, and app environment.

If you provide an email address, the other information in that feedback submission may be associated with it. The app also sends a fixed client identifier that identifies Open Caffeine as the submitting app; it does not uniquely identify your device or you.

How we use information

Feedback information is used only to receive feedback, reply to support requests, diagnose reported problems, and improve Open Caffeine. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

Service provider

Feedback and attachments are transmitted to and processed by Usero, which provides the feedback service for Open Caffeine. Usero processes this information on our behalf to deliver the feedback feature.

Retention and deletion

Feedback records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for support, product improvement, security, and applicable legal obligations. To request access to or deletion of feedback you submitted, contact us at the address below and include enough information for us to locate the submission.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect submitted information. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Open Caffeine or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@sapsaldog.com.
